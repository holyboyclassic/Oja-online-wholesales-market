#  Oja — Online Wholesale Market

**Your Marketplace for Smarter Bulk Buying!**

Oja is an online wholesale marketplace designed to connect buyers with sellers, making it easier to discover products and purchase goods in bulk. The platform aims to simplify wholesale shopping through a convenient and user-friendly online experience.

##  About the Project

Oja is a web development project focused on creating a digital marketplace where customers can explore wholesale products and connect with sellers.

The goal is to make bulk purchasing easier, faster, and more accessible while providing businesses with an online platform to showcase their products.
